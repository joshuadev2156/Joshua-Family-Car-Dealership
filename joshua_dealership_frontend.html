Who We Are

More Than Just Cars

Joshua and Family Car Dealership was established in 2009 by Joshua Mwenda and his family with a vision to bring world-class automotive service to Mombasa. Today we operate a 5-acre facility housing over 150 vehicles at any given time.

Our 35 dedicated professionals share one mission: making your car-buying journey exceptional from start to finish.
